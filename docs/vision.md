# Vision

Queer to queer. Tero is where queer people anywhere find each other, get
real help from people they can trust, and lift each other up: socially,
medically, legally and financially.

It's built like the Tarot Club: members gather "above the gray fog",
known by their card and pseudonym, not by their real identity.

## The four areas of help (the Help tab)

Plain names, no tarot suits (D41).

| Area | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Support and care** | Spaces (live audio rooms like X Spaces), group chats, posts (like X), meetings, someone to talk to (anonymous emotional support), therapy, marriage and relationship counselling |
| **Work and money** | Jobs, opportunities, queer-owned businesses (help them trend), housing, mutual aid and donations |
| **Safety and law** | Lawyers, legal help, wills the community helps uphold, safety guides, emergency help |
| **Health and growth** | Learning skills, HRT information, surgery planning (top, bottom and others), travel plans, estimates, people travelling who can bring things |

## Access levels

| Level | Who | What |
|---|---|---|
| **Public** | Anyone, no sign-in | Non-sensitive info: general health info, skills content, public guides. Never member identities or activity. |
| **Member** | Signed in and vouched for | Community, chat, directories, professionals |
| **Sequence-gated** | Members with enough trust | HRT requests, travel and couriers, wills, anything that could expose someone |

## Key features

- **Circles**: communities, each one calm conversation with topics and live Spaces (see [design.md](design.md)).
- **Spaces**: end-to-end encrypted live audio rooms like X Spaces, for communities, groups, skills and learning. Up to 6 speakers, everyone else listens. Voice disguise. Not recorded by default.
- **Voice with professionals**: private, end-to-end encrypted calls for consultations and enquiries.
- **Private audio calls**: normally between two people, with up to four participants in a group call. Spaces keep their separate six-speaker limit with additional listeners. Primary and fallback routes use the same limits.
- **Someone to talk to**: anonymous, random matching for emotional relief. Neither person knows who the other is. Only vouched members, with reporting.
- **Verified professionals**: doctors, therapists, lawyers and others, checked per country.
- **Tero partners**: clinics, pharmacies and lawyers who know what a Tero card means. A member shows their card and the partner shows the same three words back. Places that don't know Tero are clearly marked, so nobody shows their card there by mistake.
- **Resource directories**: hospitals, pharmacies, HRT providers and surgeons, with cost estimates and what to expect, per country.
- **Travel help**: someone travelling to a country posts it, and members can request help (for example legal, personal-use HRT). Trust is voted only after a confirmed handover.
- **Surgery planning**: guides, estimates and verified coordinators for any surgery, from any country to any country. Directory first; booking only with real partnerships later.
- **Wills**: write a will, with lawyers and the community helping uphold it.
- **Donations**: non-custodial stablecoin wallets with goals anyone can check (see [wallet.md](wallet.md)).

## Phases

1. **Foundation:** self-held identity keys and recovery, Tero cards, Sequences, encrypted vault, decoy passcode and duress alert, ephemeral chats, community spaces and chat, moderation, public resources, the four languages.
2. **Professionals:** verification flows and council voting, consultations.
3. **Work and money:** jobs, skills, businesses, housing, wallet.
4. **Journeys:** HRT and travel requests, surgery planning.
5. **Legal:** wills, lawyer networks, hospital partnerships, Tor route, mesh.

## On *Lord of the Mysteries*

Tero is inspired by the novel. Tarot card names and the idea of numbered
Sequences are fine to use. We don't copy the novel's text, art or specific
names (such as its pathway names).
