HRT: the basics

Reviewed by a verified doctor English · Kiswahili · 中文 · Français
This guide is general information, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before starting or changing any hormones.

What HRT is

Hormone replacement therapy, or HRT, uses hormones to bring your body's changes closer to who you are. For many trans people it is an important part of feeling at home in themselves.

Starting safely

A doctor usually begins with a conversation about your health and goals, and blood tests to know where you're starting from. Hormones bought without a prescription can be the wrong dose or not what they claim to be.

Check-ups

Regular blood tests help your doctor keep your levels in a healthy range and adjust the dose over time.

Finding care

Members of Tero share clinics and pharmacies that treat queer and trans people with respect, country by country. That directory is for members, to keep those places safe.
