How joining works

  1. 1Get a Tero card. A member who trusts you gives you one. Each card works once.
  2. 2Scan it and confirm in person. You and the member check a short code together.
  3. 3They approve you. Your keys are created on your phone. No name, email or phone number.

Built for places where it isn't safe to be seen

Messages are end-to-end encrypted. Tero never asks for your name, phone number or email, and its servers only ever hold data they can't read.
